Greetings — Ek Nazar Mein

Greetings teen categories mein baante gaye hain, yaad karna aasan ho jayega.

👋 Daily Greetings

Milte waqt (Meeting)

  • Hello / Hi
  • Good Morning — subah
  • Good Afternoon — dopahar
  • Good Evening — shaam
  • How are you?

Jaate waqt (Parting)

  • Goodbye / Bye
  • See you later
  • See you tomorrow
  • Good night
  • Take care

Shishtachar (Politeness)

  • Please
  • Thank you
  • You're welcome
  • Sorry / Excuse me
  • No problem
